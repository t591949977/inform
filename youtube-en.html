📅 What, Where, When

🔹My hobby — creating videos with artificial intelligence.

I used to shoot videos with a movie camera; the process of filming, developing film and manual editing was very complicated, took a lot of time and did not forgive mistakes. But I still enjoyed doing it. Then the age of AI came, and the possibilities of my hobby changed. Now my main working panel is the text line of the generator. I just write what is born in my head, and neural networks turn words into living, cinematic frames.
What fascinates me most about this hobby is the unpredictability and speed. I can, in one evening, "shoot" a fantastic clip about distant planets, bring old family photos to life, or create a dynamic music video for my own music. Of course, AI doesn't always understand the idea right away: sometimes characters get extra fingers, and the laws of physics in the frame are brazenly violated. But it is precisely this process of "taming" the technology, selecting the right prompts and editing the best takes that gives me enormous pleasure. For me, video generation has become the perfect way to express myself and prove that creating cinema today requires not a huge budget, but only imagination.
